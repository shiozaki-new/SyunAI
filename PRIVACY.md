# Privacy Policy - SyunAI (瞬愛)

Last updated: 2026-04-05

## Data Collection

SyunAI does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- All AI inference runs entirely on your iPhone device
- No data is sent to external servers
- No API keys or accounts are required
- No analytics or tracking is used

## Local Processing

All questions and responses are processed locally using an on-device AI model. Your data never leaves your device.
